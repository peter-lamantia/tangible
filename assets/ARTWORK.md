# Artwork attribution

The fuchsia impossible cube shown on the welcome screen is adapted from
["Impossible cube illusion angle.svg"](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Impossible_cube_illusion_angle.svg)
by **4C**, based on an earlier PNG by **Wapcaplet**. The original SVG was
recolored in fuchsia, trimmed, and rasterized for use in Tangible.

The adapted SVG (`impossible_cube_fuchsia.svg`) and its rasterized RGBA version
(`impossible_cube_fuchsia.rgba`) are licensed under
